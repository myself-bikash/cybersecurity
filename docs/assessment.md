# Phase Assessments

Use the assessment for a phase after reviewing its lessons. Each phase assessment has three parts:

1. **Prerequisite check:** identify whether the phase's prerequisite concepts are understood or need
   revision. If revision is needed, the assessment stays available, but mastery should not be
   self-assessed until those gaps are addressed.
2. **Knowledge check:** five automatically checked questions. A score of at least 4/5 (80%) passes
   this section. Review the explanations and retry if needed.
3. **Practical evidence review:** complete the phase challenge, identify a safe artifact, and rate
   it against the weighted course rubric. A score of at least 80% passes this section.

The practical score is self-assessed. The course cannot inspect or verify your work, so a recorded
pass is not a credential, certification, or independently validated claim of skill. Mastery self-assessment
requires all three: prerequisites marked ready, a passing knowledge check, and a passing practical
rubric. Use fictional, synthetic, or explicitly authorized lab material. Never enter credentials,
personal information, or confidential work evidence.

The record is stored in this browser on this device only. It is not sent to a server. Clearing
browser storage, using another browser/device, or visiting a different site origin will reset or
separate the record.

## Shared practical rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Scoring |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Conceptual understanding | 20% | Explain core ideas accurately and apply them to the scenario. |
| Technical implementation | 25% | Complete the task correctly in an authorized lab or with supplied evidence. |
| Practical problem-solving | 25% | Use evidence to choose a proportionate, safe next step. |
| Evidence and documentation | 15% | Record the artifact, relevant observations, decisions, and limitations. |
| Security reasoning and remediation | 15% | Link risk to an actionable fix and safe verification criterion. |

Each criterion is rated from 0 to 4 using the visible descriptors in the assessment form. Unmet
criteria are shown for revision. A learner may retake either section; the highest knowledge-check
score and latest practical self-assessment are retained locally.

## Retest guidance

If a section is below 80% or prerequisites need revision, use the feedback to identify a specific
gap, revisit the linked phase lessons, complete an additional practice task, then retake the
section. A passing result requires readiness and both scored sections to meet the threshold, but
only demonstrates the learner's own recorded assessment.
