# Phase 5: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

## Phase objective

Understand the principles and concepts that recur throughout cybersecurity: security principles,
cryptography, authentication, vulnerabilities and threats, and security frameworks. The phase ends
with a risk assessment of a fictional company.

## Lessons

1. [Security principles](01-security-principles.md)
2. [Cryptography](02-cryptography.md)
3. [Authentication](03-authentication.md)
4. [Vulnerabilities and threats](04-vulnerabilities-and-threats.md)
5. [Security frameworks](05-security-frameworks.md)
6. [Fictional-company risk assessment](06-risk-assessment-project.md)
7. [Final revision and assessment](07-final-revision.md)

## Progress checklist

- [ ] Explain CIA, authentication/authorization/accounting, non-repudiation, least privilege,
  defense in depth, and Zero Trust.
- [ ] Compare encryption, hashing, symmetric/asymmetric methods, AES/RSA/ECC, and related
  certificate/key concepts.
- [ ] Distinguish the authentication methods and frameworks in the source.
- [ ] Recognize each source-listed threat and its defensive measures.
- [ ] Compare the security frameworks and complete the fictional-company assessment.

## Source notes

The source cites current-named framework editions including NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls v8.1, and
OWASP Top 10:2025. These editions are retained as stated in the PDF, not silently updated. The
PDF's numbered citations do not include a bibliography.
