# Phase 00: Start Here

## Phase objective

Build a clear picture of what cybersecurity protects, why authorization matters, and how people
work together to manage risk.

## Lessons

1. [Cybersecurity and information security](01-cybersecurity-and-infosec.md)
2. [Threats, risk, and attack surface](02-threats-risk-and-attack-surface.md)
3. [Security teams, roles, and careers](03-teams-roles-and-careers.md)
4. [Case studies and exercise](04-case-studies-and-exercise.md)

## Competency checkpoint

Use a fictional organization to identify its important assets, explain one plausible risk, and
describe which team might help reduce it. State what is known, what is assumed, and what would need
authorization before any assessment.

## Progress checklist

- [ ] Distinguish cybersecurity from broader information security.
- [ ] Explain threat, vulnerability, risk, and attack surface in your own words.
- [ ] Name roles that contribute to security and the kind of work they do.
- [ ] Analyze a fictional case without treating an assumption as evidence.
- [ ] Describe why authorization matters even when intentions are good.
